# Brag Plan: Groww GenZ Activation Engine

## What is this app?
Groww GenZ is a targeted post-KYC activation engine and product architecture engineered to eliminate the 74% drop-off between KYC clearance and the first ₹500 investment, turning dormant signups into repeat investors with a 30-day habit loop.

## The angle
FinTech Product Architecture Masterclass. Instead of vague marketing fluff, this video showcases real product management rigour: identifying why small-ticket equity buying fails (the ₹15.93 CDSL depository fee trap), replacing a 5,000+ mutual fund catalog with goal-based pots, mathematically verifying compounding fee drag, and engineering "The First Red Day Defense" to prevent panic dormancy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"74% of GenZ drop off immediately after KYC clearance." A bold metric badge and high-contrast typography set the stakes before revealing why novice investors freeze.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Pots, Not Products:** The 5,000-fund mutual fund catalog is replaced with intent-based buckets (Safety Net, Goa Trip, New Phone, Long Run) with a ₹500 entry floor.
- **Compounding Fee Drag Engine:** Mathematically proving real daily NAV drag vs naive principal models (23/23 unit test assertions verified).
- **The First Red Day Defense:** Normalizing market drawdowns on Day 1 to stop panic churn before it starts.
- **30-Day Frictionless Habit:** Screen 10 greets returning users with "Welcome back. We are not asking you everything again" for 1-tap re-investment.

## Outro / punchline
"Groww GenZ: Post-KYC Activation & 30-Day Habit Engine · Product Strategy & Architecture by Prathamesh Deshmane · Live on Vercel."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** User selects an Intent Pot ("Emergency Pot", ₹500) instead of scrolling 5,000 funds.
2. **Key action:** Dynamic compounding cost breakdown reveals ₹0.51 total expense vs SBI FD baseline.
3. **Result:** First Red Day psychological guardrail and 1-tap 30-day repeat habit unlocked.
