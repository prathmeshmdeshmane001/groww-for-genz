# Share Copy Variants: Groww GenZ

### 1. LinkedIn (Detailed PM Case Study)
74% of first-time retail investors drop off immediately after KYC clearance.

Why? Novices don’t fail from lack of capital — they freeze when handed a 5,000+ mutual fund catalog with zero guidance, while flat ₹15.93 CDSL depository charges silently destroy small-ticket equity buying on Day 1.

I designed **Groww GenZ**: a post-KYC activation engine and product architecture centered on one North Star: *2nd Investment within 30 Days*.

Key pillars:
1. **Pots, Not Products**: Replaced the mutual fund catalog with 4 goal-based intent buckets (Safety Net, Goa Trip, New Gadget, Long Run). Horizon automatically sets asset allocation.
2. **Deterministic Compounding Drag Engine**: Proved why a ₹500 ticket only works with liquid/index funds (₹0.51 fee drag vs ₹18 stock depository drag; 23/23 assertions verified).
3. **The First Red Day Defense**: Normalizing the first -4.2% market correction on Day 1 with historical recovery context to prevent panic dormancy.
4. **Frictionless Habit Loop**: 1-tap re-engagement for returning users.

Check out the interactive case study & iPhone 17 prototype live:
