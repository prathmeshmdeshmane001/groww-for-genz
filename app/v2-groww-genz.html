What if it doesn't come back?

Sometimes it doesn't, for a while. After the 2008 crash the Nifty took about three years to get back. In March 2020 it fell about 38% in a month and took about nine months.

Most falls come back inside a year. Some take much longer. That is the pattern, not a promise. It is why we asked when you need the money.

What we left out

Four things, and the reason for each.

F&O and intraday

43% of people trading F&O in India are under 30. 89% of them lost money last year. They put up 43% of the turnover and took 53% of the losses.

Personal loans

Groww's own Play Store listing offers loans at 13–48% a year, on the same screen as investing. We are not putting a 48% loan next to someone's first ₹500.

Google Play listing, com.nextbillion.groww, read 16 Sep 2026
"Most bought" and "trending" lists

Groww shows Most Bought Options, Volume Shockers and screens called Resistance Breakouts and RSI Overbought. A list of what went up last week is not advice, and it is how people buy at the top.
