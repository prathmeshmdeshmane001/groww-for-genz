    What if it doesn't come back?

    Sometimes it doesn't, for a while. After the 2008 crash the Nifty took about three years to get back. In March 2020 it fell about 38% in a month and took about nine months.

    Most falls recover inside a year. Some take much longer. That's the pattern, not a promise.

    This is why we asked when you need the money. Three years gives a fall room to undo itself. Six months doesn't.
