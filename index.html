System Architecture & Strategic Pillars

Product Principles
Information Architecture
Pots, Not Products
Replaces the 5,000+ instrument mutual fund and equity catalog with intent-based buckets (Safety Net, Trip, Gadget, Long Run). Horizon determines asset allocation automatically.
Deterministic Engine
Real Compounding Cost Drag
Rejects naive principal calculations. Dynamically models daily NAV fee drag compounding on growing balances (+53% higher than static models) and benchmarks against SBI FDs.
Psychological Guardrail
The First Red Day Defense
87% of dormancy is driven by immediate market drawdowns. Screen 9 normalizes a -4.2% correction with historical recovery data (2008 & 2020) and habituates holding.
Retention Loop
30-Day Frictionless Habit
Screen 10 eliminates re-onboarding questionnaires on return visits. Greets user with "Welcome back. We are not asking you everything again" with a 1-tap repeat top-up.
